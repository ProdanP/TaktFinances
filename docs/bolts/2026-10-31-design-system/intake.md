## Summary

This bolt sets the app theme and applies it to the current shell. Android and desktop share a Material 3 theme in `:sharedUI`, taken from the Material Theme Builder export at `docs/bolts/2026-10-31-design-system/material-theme.json` (seed `#769CDF`), including its light, dark, and contrast schemes. iOS uses that same export as the color source, expressed with SwiftUI semantic colors, the system font, and system materials.
