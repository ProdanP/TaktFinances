# Intake

## Depth

feature

## Summary

This bolt adds the Landing screen as the first screen of onboarding, replacing the template greeting on Android, desktop, and iOS. The screen shows a short welcome, a money-themed illustration drafted in the project Figma file, and a Continue button at the bottom that does nothing when tapped. The onboarding feature module holds the domain; Compose lives in `:sharedUI` and SwiftUI lives in `iosApp`.
